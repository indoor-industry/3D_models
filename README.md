Collection of 3D designs for repair of various objects, at the moment of writing this includes:
-Fiat 130 brake regulator arms bushings
-A simple replacement for the holder for the idle control valve of electronic injection-fitted Golf mk2's 
-Modified central dashboard piece of a Nissan Patrol with additional gauge holder and trays
-Seat and chassis cover plastics for a Beta Techno trial bike
-Some random stuff like Kenwood turntable lid hinge parts and curtain holder rings
